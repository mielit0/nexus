Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

NEXUS Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the NEXUS TikTok account.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the tool reads basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and uploads content to the owner's account as drafts.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No personal data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Images placed in the public media folder of this site are the carousel images meant to be published.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings (Security and permissions, Apps).
